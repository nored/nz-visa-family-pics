fperoman-regular.ttf, fperoman-bold.ttf and fperoman-italic.ttf are derived from
Latin Modern Roman 10, version 2.004 (lmroman10-regular.otf, lmroman10-bold.otf,
lmroman10-italic.otf, from CTAN fonts/lm). The CFF outlines were converted to
TrueType quadratic outlines with fontTools so that pdf-lib can embed and subset
them. Following clause 1 of the GUST Font License, the fonts and files were renamed.
