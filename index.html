    Privacy and storage

    The app runs in your browser. Photos and descriptions are stored in this browser only (IndexedDB), so you can close the page and continue later on the same device. Nothing is sent to a server; the PDF is built on your phone or laptop.

    On Android, install the app (browser menu, "Add to Home screen" or "Install app") and it appears in the share sheet of your gallery. On iPhone, use Add photos, which opens the photo library.
